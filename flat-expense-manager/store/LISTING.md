## Full description
```
Flat Expense Manager keeps your flat's shared spending simple and fair.

• Add an expense in seconds: what it was, how much, and who paid
• Split it equally with the people who share it, or enter exact amounts
• See everyone's balance: who gets money back and who owes
• Settle up with the fewest payments: "Bilal pays Ali Rs. 1,200"
• Record full or partial payments between flatmates
• Every flatmate logs in on their own phone, and records sync instantly
• Only the person who added an entry can change or delete it
• Works offline for viewing; nothing is ever lost
• Daily, weekly, monthly and per-member reports
• Download a PDF report of all expenses
• Pakistani Rupees (Rs.), with exact paisa calculations

Made for students and working people sharing a flat. No ads. No tracking.
```
